How Rise Joe E-Commerce Agency collects and uses the information you share through this website.

This notice explains how Rise Joe E-Commerce Agency ("we", "us") handles personal information submitted through this website. Please review it with your legal adviser before publishing; it is a starting template.

What we collect

Only the information you choose to submit in our enquiry forms: your name, email, optional phone/WhatsApp number, country, and the details relevant to the service you are enquiring about.

What we never ask for

We never request marketplace passwords, OTP codes, verification codes or other sensitive login credentials. Please do not include them in any form.

How we use it

  • To respond to your enquiry and route it to the responsible team
  • To send you an acknowledgement and follow-up about your request
  • To keep a basic record of the enquiry

Data minimisation

We ask only for what each service needs. We do not sell your information or share it with third parties for marketing.

Retention

Enquiry information is kept only as long as needed to handle your request and any resulting relationship.

Your choices

You may ask us to access, correct or delete your information by contacting admin@risejoe.com.
