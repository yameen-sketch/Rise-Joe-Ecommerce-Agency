Two pathways.One destination.

Your situation decides the route, not your potential. Both pathways lead to real, practical e-commerce experience.

Path A · Business launchA

Launch your own e-commerce venture

For students with access to international business connections and/or investment who want to plan, establish and operate their own e-commerce business.

  • We help plan and set up your business, in Pakistan or overseas
  • Complete guidance on marketplace, products and operations
  • Ongoing support as you establish and run your venture
  • Access to our sourcing, logistics and account expertise
Apply for Path A
Path B · Practical workB

Learn through real projects

For students without investment or overseas connections who want to learn through supervised internal projects and potential agency work.

  • Join internal projects as a trainee
  • Gain real, hands-on e-commerce experience
  • Work alongside our experienced team
  • Potential agency work, subject to evaluation and availability
